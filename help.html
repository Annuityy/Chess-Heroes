Help

Chess for kids, with six toy heroes. Here is how to keep it on a phone and play anywhere.

Keep it on a phone or tablet

The game can live on the Home Screen like an app: its own icon, the whole screen, and no internet needed.

  • Android (Chrome): open this address, then tap Install. If no button shows, open the menu (the three dots) and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  • iPhone and iPad (Safari): open this address, tap Share (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  • A laptop (Chrome or Edge): click the install button at the right end of the address bar.

No internet? It still plays

After the first visit, the whole game is kept on the device: every level, mission, lesson and game. A new version arrives by itself the next time the game is opened with internet.

Sound

The speaker at the top of every screen turns all sound off and on. Each player's sounds, music, the coach's voice and helpers are in Coach's Corner, under the player's settings.

Coach's Corner, for grown-ups

On Who's playing?, tap Coach's Corner and answer the grown-up question. Inside: each player's progress, settings and hero certificates, and the switches Knows chess and Open everything.

Players

Each player has a card with their own look, stars and levels. To change a player's picture or delete a player, tap the pencil on their card. Players are kept only on this device.
